Lucky Rush:
Dimensional Survivor

Run through disaster zones, dodge collapsing structures, and collect lucky numbers. Fortune can grant abilities, shields, and temporary invincibility; misfortune can send you into a monster-filled alternate dimension.

Defeat every enemy before the countdown ends to escape and return to your run. Every run is a new gamble between fortune and misfortune.
